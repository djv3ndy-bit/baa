# BaristaMatch Google Play listing draft

This draft is for the planned Android release with Pro purchases. Confirm the signed Android candidate supports these features before submitting it.

## App name

BaristaMatch

## Short description

Find café jobs, connect with baristas, and manage hiring in one place

## Full description

BaristaMatch connects baristas and cafés looking for their next match.

For baristas:

- Create a profile with your skills, experience, and availability.
- Discover café jobs and send applications.
- Follow your matches and message cafés through the app.

For cafés:

- Create your café profile and post job opportunities.
- Review candidates and find baristas who fit your team.
- Manage applications, matches, and conversations in one place.

Keep your profile up to date, follow notifications, and stay connected as your search or hiring needs change.

BaristaMatch Pro is an optional monthly subscription for café accounts. Available features, pricing, and subscription terms are shown in the app before purchase. Manage a Google Play subscription through your Google Play account.

An account is required to use marketplace features. Job availability and matches depend on participating cafés and baristas.
