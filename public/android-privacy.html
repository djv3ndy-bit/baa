Privacy Policy for BaristaMatch on Android

Effective October 12, 2026.

BaristaMatch LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("BaristaMatch," "we," "us," or "our"), operates a hiring marketplace connecting baristas and cafés. This policy explains how we handle information when you use BaristaMatch on Android and its related account, support, and subscription services.

We use personal information to operate accounts, profiles, job posts, applications, matching, messaging, notifications, subscriptions, safety, and support. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party targeted advertising.

1. Information you provide

Accounts and sign-in. We process your email address, account type, name or café name, account identifier, and sign-in/session information. Email/password sign-in sends the password you enter to our authentication provider, Supabase. Where an optional Google or Apple sign-in is available and you choose it, we receive the identity information supplied for that sign-in, such as an account identifier, email address, and name. Do not include your password in profile text, messages, reports, or support requests.

Profiles and jobs. We collect the information you enter for your profile and hiring or work preferences, including general location, café or job address, biography, skills, experience, availability, desired pay, café details, and job descriptions. Some information is needed to complete a profile or use a feature. A profile photo is required for discovery readiness; additional café imagery and a coffee showcase video are optional.

Private age and demographic information. Barista profile completion requires a date of birth for minimum-age eligibility and aggregate age reporting. Gender is optional and used only for aggregate reporting. You may leave gender unprovided or remove a previous selection by choosing Prefer not to say. Not providing gender does not affect visibility, matching, or access. Date of birth, calculated age, and gender are stored separately from the marketplace profile and are not shown to cafés or other marketplace members.

Photos and videos. When you select media, we upload and store the selected files. Current Android builds use the system picker to select files instead of requesting broad media-library access. Selected files can contain embedded metadata, including location information, and videos can contain audio. We do not promise that uploading a file removes its original metadata. Avoid uploading sensitive information you do not want included in your selected media.

Marketplace activity and communications. We store applications, interests, matches, messages, conversation participants, timestamps, read status, notifications, and related job or profile references. We also process information you submit in a safety report, support request, or account-deletion request.

2. Technical and usage information

We record account and product activity needed to operate and improve the service, including profile views, the café associated with a view, application and matching activity, screen or page activity, and login/security events. Error and support records can include error details, page or request paths, browser or app information, and diagnostic context.

Our hosting, authentication, notification, and delivery providers also process technical information in providing their services. Depending on the service, this can include IP addresses, request times and status, user-agent or device/app information, installation identifiers, and delivery or security logs. These records are separate from information shown on your marketplace profile.

The reviewed Android app uses manually entered location information for matching and job details. Selected media can contain location metadata even when the app does not request a device GPS location.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and maintain accounts.
  • Display relevant profiles and job posts and apply your saved work or hiring preferences.
  • Process applications, candidate review, interests, matches, and private conversations.
  • Show baristas which cafés viewed their profile.
  • Deliver service notifications and enabled transactional emails.
  • Verify subscription access, restore purchases, prevent duplicate subscriptions, and handle billing events.
  • Provide time-limited review access where specifically assigned.
  • Respond to support, privacy, deletion, and safety requests.
  • Troubleshoot reliability, understand product use, prevent abuse or fraud, and protect users and the service.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and address payment disputes or other claims.

4. Visibility and other marketplace members

Signed-in marketplace members can see information intended for matching, such as your display name or café name, general location, biography, skills, experience, availability, desired pay, profile media, and job posts. A barista can see the café name and visit time when a café opens the barista's profile. Applications, interests, matches, and messages are made available to the relevant participants as needed for those features.

You can switch off profile discovery in your profile controls. Doing so does not delete your account, existing conversations, applications, or matches. Profile and café images use publicly accessible file URLs; hiding a profile does not make a previously obtained image URL private or remove copies someone has already saved. Coffee showcase videos use authenticated, time-limited access rather than a permanently public video URL.

Messages are processed and stored by BaristaMatch and its service providers so the conversation works. They are not represented as end-to-end encrypted. A recipient may retain information you send them outside BaristaMatch.

5. Android notifications

On supported Android configurations, we use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications. We associate the device notification token with your BaristaMatch account, app/platform information, and registration dates. Notification data contains the recipient account identifier and references needed to open the relevant app screen. Delivery records can include event and recipient references, a token fingerprint, delivery or retry status, and timestamps; the delivery queue does not store message bodies or raw push tokens. Firebase also processes installation and technical information for messaging.

Direct Android private-message alerts use generic text instead of the sender's name or message body. Other alerts may include profile, match, or job information. Full message content remains in the in-app conversation and may also be included in enabled email notifications. Other installed versions or configurations can use Expo for push delivery; those paths can carry notification text and message previews. An upgraded installation may resume an earlier Expo registration during startup before direct Android notification setup completes.

You can manage notification permission in Android settings. Turning off visible notifications does not necessarily stop every underlying SDK installation identifier or technical request. Logging out removes the account's direct notification registration and attempts to invalidate its native push token. Notifications already accepted, queued, or displayed can remain with the provider or on your device.

6. Subscriptions and limited reviewer access

For an Android café subscription purchased through Google Play, Google processes the payment. We receive purchase and subscription information such as purchase tokens, order/transaction and plan identifiers, account binding, status, renewal information, and subscription dates. We associate these records with your BaristaMatch account to verify access, restore purchases, prevent duplicate subscriptions, and process renewal, cancellation, refund, and other billing events. We do not receive your full payment-card number or Google Account password through this purchase flow.

Where your account already has a website or other supported store subscription, we may also check its associated billing status to keep access consistent and prevent conflicting subscriptions. Stripe administers supported website subscriptions; this does not mean an Android Google Play purchase is processed through Stripe.

We may assign selected reviewer accounts limited, non-renewing Pro access without a purchase. For those accounts, we keep the account identifier, grant creation, expiry and revocation information, and administrative approval records. The app identifies this as Reviewer Pro access and displays its expiry. It is not a paid Google Play or Stripe subscription and does not create purchase history. Actual subscription records, if any, remain separate.

Deleting the app or your BaristaMatch account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel renewal separately in Google Play. Other store subscriptions must likewise be managed with the relevant store.

7. Providers and other disclosures

We use providers for the following purposes:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, realtime updates, and file storage.
  • Vercel: hosting, API processing, delivery, and operational logging.
  • Google Firebase/FCM: Android notification delivery and related installation/technical processing.
  • Expo: the application platform, build services, and notification processing in applicable versions/configurations.
  • Resend: enabled transactional and support email delivery, which can include recipient details and relevant profile, job, application, match, or message content.
  • Google Play: Android purchases and subscription verification/events.
  • Google and Apple: optional sign-in, where available and chosen.
  • Stripe: supported website billing and related subscription administration/status checks.

These providers process the information needed for the services used. Their own terms and privacy notices can also apply to services they provide directly to you or to independently processed service/security information. Provider capabilities and retention settings vary; deleting a BaristaMatch account does not establish immediate deletion of every provider log or backup.

We disclose information to marketplace counterparts as described above. We may also disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, security, or safety. Providers may process information in countries other than where you live.

8. Local storage

The app stores session/authentication information and notification-registration state on your device to keep you signed in and operate core features. Related web pages use browser storage and similar necessary technologies. Device permissions and your device's app-storage controls provide additional choices; clearing local storage or uninstalling the app does not itself delete your server account or cancel a subscription.

9. Retention and account deletion

We keep account and marketplace information while your account is active and only as necessary to provide the service. Limited records may remain after account deletion where needed for billing verification, payment disputes, security or fraud prevention, legal obligations, or resolving a support/safety matter. How long a record is needed depends on the account or transaction, any unresolved support or safety matter, applicable legal obligations, and the technical retention of the service involved. We do not promise one fixed retention period for every category.

Deletion from the app. Sign in, open Settings, expand Advanced settings, select Delete my account, and follow the confirmation steps. The deletion process removes the account identity and associated profile, private demographics, owned jobs, applications/matches/messages, uploaded media, notification registrations, and reviewer grant rows covered by the account-deletion relationships. Pending Android alerts are no longer eligible when the source account or conversation is removed; minimal delivery records can remain until queue maintenance removes them. Deletion is permanent when completed.

Deletion without the app. Visit Delete your BaristaMatch account or email hello@baristajobmatch.com from your account email with the subject BaristaMatch account deletion request, confirming that you want the account and associated personal data deleted. You can also use the website support form. We verify ownership, explain any additional verification or retention requirements, and confirm completion. An email request does not delete an account instantly. Do not send passwords, verification codes, government identification numbers, or payment-card details.

Records that are not automatically removed with the account. Limited purchase/account-binding, verification, billing-event and checkout records can remain for the purposes above. Support/contact content and some safety records can remain. Some product/error and safety records have their direct user reference removed rather than the entire record being deleted; free text or other context may still contain personal information. Expired or revoked reviewer grant rows remain until account deletion or administrative removal, and separate reviewer approval configuration needs administrative cleanup. Invalid notification registrations may be disabled before removal; account deletion removes account-linked registrations.

Provider copies and recipients. Provider logs, emails, backup copies, and cached public images are subject to the applicable service and configuration. They may persist after removal from the live account. Copies already received or saved by another user, and notifications already delivered, are not recalled by deleting your account. Retention of these copies depends on their purpose, the provider, and the applicable service settings.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel store subscriptions; follow the subscription instructions above. If you used Sign in with Apple and an authorization token is unavailable, we can provide instructions to disconnect Apple after deleting the BaristaMatch account; automatic Apple revocation is not promised.

10. Your choices, rights, and security

You can edit profile information, manage discovery visibility and optional media, manage notification permissions, and block or report users. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information, or object to certain processing. Contact us using the details below. We respond to verified requests as required by applicable law.

We use authentication, access controls, database row policies, private video access, file restrictions, and encrypted network connections designed to protect information. These measures do not guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password and keep your login credentials private.

11. Younger users

BaristaMatch is intended for people aged 16 and older, including users aged 16–17 and adults. Account creation requires confirmation of minimum age, and barista profile completion requires a private date of birth. Users below the age of majority may use the service only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. Cafés and users remain responsible for applicable work-eligibility, permitted-hours, and consent requirements. If you believe someone under 16 has created an account, contact us so we can investigate and delete the information as appropriate.

We may update this policy as the Android app and related services change. We will post a revised effective date and provide additional notice when required.

BaristaMatch LLC is responsible for this policy. For privacy, access, deletion, or younger-user concerns, contact hello@baristajobmatch.com or support@baristajobmatch.com.
