How it works

Swipe. Match. Chat. Get hired.

1. Create a profile

Baristas add experience, availability, skills, and expected pay. Coffee shops add their open role, schedule, and culture.

2. Show interest

Each side reviews focused cards instead of searching through a crowded general job board.

3. Match and interview

Mutual interest opens chat and interview scheduling.
