Disconnect Sign in with Apple

If you used Sign in with Apple, deleting your BaristaMatch account and disconnecting Apple are separate actions. Follow these instructions after your account deletion is confirmed.

  1. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings and tap your name. Choose Sign in with Apple.
  2. Select BaristaMatch or its developer and follow the option to stop using Sign in with Apple.
  3. On Android or a computer, sign in at account.apple.com, open Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, and select BaristaMatch.

If BaristaMatch is not listed, Apple may already be disconnected. Read Apple's official instructions.
